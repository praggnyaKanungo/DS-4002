# Data Dictionary For Hot Dog Survey Dataset

## Columns

| Column name | Type | Legal values / range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| timestamp | integer | month/day/year, hour:minute:second | Indicates the month and time | 
| date | date | YYYY-MM-DD | Date survey was taken |
| is_sandwich| boolean | yes/no (or 0/1) | Whether respondent thinks hot dogs are sandwiches |
| how frequently | string | frequently, rarely, occasionally, never| How frequently the respondent eats hot dogs |
| year_at_UVA | string | 1st year, 2nd year, 3rd year, 4th year, Graduate Student, Faculty/Staff, Other/Not Affiliated| the year of the student at UVA | 
| gender | string | Man, Woman, Nonbinary, Prefer not to say  | gender of the respondent | 
| school | string | Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Darden School of Business, Batten School of Public Policy, McIntire School of Commerce, Architecture, Data Science, Education, Law, Medicine, Nursing, Continuing and Professional Studies  | school of attendance of the respondent |
